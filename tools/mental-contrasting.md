---
name: Creative visualisation, done properly
family: exit
order: 2
when: You're picturing the good version, like the talk that goes well or the changed life, and the picture feels like progress.
move: Picture the outcome, then right away picture the main obstacle inside you, then plan for it. If the obstacle shows up, you'll do one specific thing. The sequence is wish, outcome, obstacle, plan.
ask: The obstacle inside you, and your if-then for it.
---
Positive fantasy on its own predicts less effort and worse results. Part of the mind treats the imagined success as though it has already happened. Mental contrasting imagines the outcome and then the obstacle in its way. That turns the same imagery into energy and a plan, and it pairs well with if-then planning.

Picture the obstacle as vividly as the prize.
