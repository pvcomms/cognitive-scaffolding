---
name: Telescoping, so date it
family: vantage
order: 3
when: You hear yourself say "always", "forever" or "only the other day".
move: Put real dates on it. When did it start? When did it last happen? How many times in the last three months?
ask: The actual dates, and the actual count.
---
Memory misdates events. Forward telescoping pulls old, vivid events closer in time. A painful thing from two years ago feels recent, and events from outside a period get counted inside it, so how often something happens is overstated.

Checking a calendar, your messages or a journal is cheap, and it usually takes the loop down a notch. You can check a pattern once it has dates. You can't check "always".
