---
name: Essentialist bias
family: shortcut
order: 1
when: You're describing yourself or someone else as a kind of person, like "a ruminator", "not a social person" or "they're just like that".
move: Rewrite the kind as a behaviour with a context. Not "I'm a withdrawer" but "I withdrew after the last three launches."
ask: Rewrite the label as a behaviour, with a when and a where.
---
Psychological essentialism is the intuition that a category has a hidden inner essence that makes its members what they are. It develops early in childhood, and it works well for tigers and water. Applied to people, including you, it turns a pattern into a nature. Natures don't change, so why try?

There's a tension in this toolbox worth saying out loud. "The type of person who does interventions" is also a type. The way out is to hold the type as a record of behaviour rather than an essence: a type you vote for, one act at a time.
