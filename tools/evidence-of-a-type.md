Self-perception theory says that when our inner states are weak or ambiguous, we infer our attitudes from watching our own behaviour, the way an outsider would. Behavioural activation treats low mood and withdrawal the same way, from the outside in. You act on a plan rather than a mood, and the mood tends to follow.

You don't think your way into being the type of person who intervenes. You collect the evidence.
