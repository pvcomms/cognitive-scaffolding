name: Augmentation, the AI rule
family: screen
order: 2
when: You're about to ask a model to think it through for you.
move: Let the model ask, not answer. It can question, steelman, retrieve and check. It doesn't reach the verdict, and it doesn't write the intervention.
ask: What did you hand the model, and which part did you keep?
---
Engelbart's 1962 framework was called *augmenting human intellect*. The aim was to raise what a person can do, not to stand in for them. Offloading thinking onto tools is normal and often smart. But in a survey of knowledge workers, more confidence in generative AI went with less critical thinking, and more self-confidence went with more of it.

The sparring brief in the run puts this into practice: the model questions, you decide, and the act stays yours.
