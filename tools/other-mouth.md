---
name: Put the words in someone else's mouth
family: vantage
order: 1
when: You can't tell whether the thought is right or just loud.
move: Give the thought to someone else. If a friend said it about themselves, what would you tell them? If someone you dislike made the argument, would you still accept it?
ask: Whose mouth did you put it in, and what changed?
---
This has two uses. The first is self-distancing. People reason more wisely about a friend's problem than their own (Solomon's paradox), and the gap closes when they take a third-person view of themselves. Even using your own name in self-talk helps.

The second is a source swap. Hand an argument to a speaker you like or dislike, and you see how much of your judgment was really about the speaker.
