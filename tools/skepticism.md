---
name: Skepticism, pointed inward
family: argument
order: 3
when: A thought feels true and you haven't checked it, because it's yours.
move: Treat the thought as a claim someone handed you. Ask what would make you believe it, and what would show it's false.
ask: What evidence would change your mind, and have you gone looking for it?
---
You'd never accept a stranger's claim on the evidence you accept for your own 2 a.m. thoughts. Metacognitive awareness means relating to thoughts as events in the mind rather than readouts of reality. It is one of the mechanisms linked to lower relapse in mindfulness-based cognitive therapy for depression.

This isn't cynicism. It's asking your own thoughts for the same evidence you'd ask of anyone else's.
