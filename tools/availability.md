---
name: Availability, where vivid isn't frequent
family: shortcut
order: 2
when: One scene keeps replaying and it's standing in for the whole picture.
move: Ask how often, not how vivid. List the last five times this situation came up, not the worst one.
ask: The last five times this came up, how did each one go?
---
We judge how common or likely something is by how easily examples come to mind. Vivid, recent and emotional events come to mind easily, and a bad event outweighs a good one of the same size. So the worst party stands in for every party.

The fix is mechanical. Take a sample instead of relying on recall.
