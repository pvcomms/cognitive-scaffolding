---
name: Divorce the belief from the body
family: vantage
order: 2
when: The certainty is strong and physical, felt in the chest, jaw or stomach, and the evidence is thin.
move: First name the body's state and its likely cause. Then look at the belief again with the feeling set to one side.
ask: What is the body doing right now, and what caused that, apart from the belief?
---
We read our feelings as information about whatever is in front of us. In Schwarz and Clore's study, people rated their whole lives as worse on rainy days. When the researchers mentioned the weather, the effect went away. Naming the cause of the feeling detached it from the judgment.

The sense of certainty is also a feeling, not a proof. Hunger, tiredness, loneliness, a medication wearing off, too much noise: name the cause and it stops voting.
