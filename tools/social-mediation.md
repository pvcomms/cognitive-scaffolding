Much of what we believe about ourselves arrives socially. When there's no objective measure, we judge ourselves by comparison with others, and we choose who counts as the comparison, often without noticing that we chose. Asch showed how hard a group's stated view pulls on a person's report of something as plain as the length of a line.

Every belief was socially given at some point, so that's not the question. The question is whether you'd pick that source now.
