name: If-then
family: exit
order: 3
when: You know what you should do. You've known it for weeks.
move: Tie the action to a cue. When this situation happens, I will do that. The situation does the remembering.
ask: When this happens, I will do that. Write both halves.
---
Implementation intentions hand control of an action to a cue in the situation. Starting stops depending on remembering to, or on feeling like it. A meta-analysis of 94 studies found that forming them had a medium-to-large effect on reaching goals (d = .65).

That's why the intervention step in every run is an if-then.
