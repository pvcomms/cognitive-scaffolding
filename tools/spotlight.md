---
name: The spotlight is dimmer than it feels
family: others
order: 1
when: You're replaying what they must have noticed, like the stumble, the pause or the thing you said.
move: Discount your estimate. Other people noticed less of your slip, and saw less of your nerves, than you think.
ask: What did they probably notice? Now halve it.
---
In Gilovich's studies, students made to wear an embarrassing T-shirt into a room guessed that about half the people there noticed it. About a quarter did. The illusion of transparency is the companion finding: people overestimate how much of their nervousness others can see.

Both biases are egocentric. You're the only person watching the whole replay.
